Data Retention & Deletion

SPR retains information only for legitimate service, security, billing, audit and support purposes described in its Privacy Policy and applicable agreements.

Customer-controlled data

Customer requests to delete workspace data are handled through the service's available deletion workflow and applicable contractual terms. Some records may need to be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, billing, legal obligations, dispute resolution or audit integrity.

Evidence and audit records

Evidence records and audit trails are designed to preserve provenance and integrity. Deletion or modification may therefore be constrained where an immutable or integrity-protected record is required by the product's evidence model or applicable law.

Backups

Deleted production data may persist temporarily in provider backups until those backups expire according to the configured infrastructure retention schedule. SPR does not promise immediate physical erasure from every backup copy.

Request deletion

Send a request to contact@softwarepassportregistry.com with “Privacy — Deletion Request” in the subject. Do not include passwords, API keys or other secrets.

Need a contractual retention period?

MSP and enterprise customers should request the applicable retention terms before onboarding sensitive data. Contractual commitments control where they differ from this general description.
