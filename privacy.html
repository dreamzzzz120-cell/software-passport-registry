SPR Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026

Information we process

SPR may process account identity information, tenant and organization information, software metadata, security findings, evidence, audit records, billing metadata and information supplied through connected integrations.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, isolate tenants, provide software-trust analysis, operate monitoring and governance features, process billing, prevent abuse, maintain auditability, support customers and improve reliability.

Customer-controlled data

Customers are responsible for ensuring that connected sources and submitted information may lawfully be processed. SPR provides tenant-scoped controls and retention settings for supported records.

Security

SPR uses authentication controls, tenant isolation, database row-level security, audit trails, rate limiting and security monitoring. No system can eliminate all security risk.

Retention and deletion

Supported tenants can configure retention policies and request workspace deletion. Certain immutable security, audit or evidentiary records may be retained where necessary to preserve integrity, security or legal obligations.

Service providers

SPR may use infrastructure and service providers necessary to operate hosting, authentication, billing, communications, monitoring and storage. Their processing is limited to providing those services.

Your requests

Where applicable, customers can submit access, correction, deletion, portability or objection requests through the privacy-management capabilities of the service.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current published version.

Contact

Privacy questions should be directed to the SPR service operator through the contact method published in the application.
