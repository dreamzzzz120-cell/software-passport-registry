# MSP Client Services — What You Can Sell with SPR

> **Important:** These are potential MSP-managed services, not turnkey SPR-certified compliance services. Charge for actual, authorized work delivered. SPR can support evidence collection and explanation, but does not itself prove legal compliance, remove the need for human review, or guarantee savings. Validate each advertised product against operational capabilities and agreements before launch.

## Laws and frameworks: applicability-first mapping

| Law / framework | Who may need it | Useful MSP service | What SPR can evidence | What remains outside SPR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **BC PIPA** (law) | Most BC private-sector organizations processing personal information | Privacy-aware software/vendor review | Observed software inventory, evidence lineage, documented missing information | Lawful purposes, consent, access requests, policies, organizational safeguards |
| **PIPEDA** (Canadian federal private-sector privacy law) | Organizations and activities within PIPEDA jurisdiction | Evidence register, third-party risk review | Documented observations and software change history | Whether the legislation applies, full safeguards, incident notification and accountability |
| **CASL** (Canadian anti-spam law) | Organizations sending commercial electronic messages | Outreach process and evidence review | Evidence about recorded consent/suppression *only when supported by actual connected records* | Proving each contact's valid consent/basis, identification, unsubscribe and legal exceptions |
| **HIPAA Security Rule** (US regulation) | Covered entities and business associates where applicable | Technical evidence readiness and gap reports | Source-linked technical observations, known limitations | ePHI scope, risk analysis, BAAs, administrative/physical safeguards, full compliance |
| **FTC Safeguards Rule** (US regulation) | Covered financial institutions | Vendor monitoring evidence and risk documentation | Supplier/software evidence and change observations | Complete written security program, risk governance, testing and statutory coverage |
| **NIST CSF 2.0** (voluntary framework) | Any organization choosing to adopt it | Control gap assessment support | Evidence mapping to selected Identify/Protect/Detect activities | Implementation and conformance decisions |
| **SOC 2** (attestation, not law) | Service providers whose customers request an independent audit | Auditor-ready evidence preparation | Source-linked supporting evidence and unresolved gaps | Independent auditor testing and opinion |
| **ISO/IEC 27001** (certifiable management-system standard, not law) | Organizations pursuing certification | Annex A evidence collection and controls register | Observed technical evidence | ISMS scope, governance, risk treatment and accredited certification |

Only claim a regulation applies after considering jurisdiction, sector, activity and data types. Never label a report compliant, HIPAA-certified, SOC-2-certified or ISO-certified solely on the basis of an SPR scan.

## Approved sales language
**Sell the work, not a certification.** “Turn software and vendor reviews into evidence-backed client reports, document what was checked, surface unresolved risks, and make follow-up services easier to deliver.”

## Pilot acceptance
- Validate one real public-repository report, plain-English explanation, PDF and safe sharing.
- Obtain client authorization for assets and any sensitive data analyzed.
- Demonstrate only features verified on production; unknowns remain UNKNOWN.
- Measure baseline/report preparation time and customer-assisted time on comparable tasks.
- Track each business prospect through verified contact, meeting, pilot, delivery, and separately paid invoice with consent and suppression rules.
