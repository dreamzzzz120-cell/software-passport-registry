SPR Legal & Trust Notice

Published August 31, 2026

Evidence-first positioning

Software Passport Registry is an evidence and identity platform. Its outputs are based on observed or supplied evidence and identify limitations where evidence is incomplete, stale or unavailable.

No certification or warranty

An SPR passport, score, report, badge or finding is not a security certification, legal opinion, compliance certification, warranty, insurance product or guarantee of future software behavior.

Customer responsibility

Customers remain responsible for authorization to inspect connected systems, the accuracy of supplied information, procurement decisions, regulatory obligations and the actions taken in response to SPR findings.

Third-party services

SPR may depend on third-party infrastructure, authentication, billing, communication, source-code, vulnerability and storage providers. Their availability and terms may affect particular capabilities.

Responsible disclosure

Security issues should be reported through the security contact published by the service operator. Do not attempt to access another customer's data or intentionally disrupt the service while investigating a suspected issue.

See the published Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for the governing service terms and privacy practices.
